Settings look: Automatic matches your phone (coloured circles on Pixel, Samsung and Xiaomi; plain icons in a flat list on Sony, Motorola and others), or pick Pixel or Plain. "Order of lines" is now on the main settings page. Work-profile apps get a dot on the icon, an asterisk after the name, or both. Fixed reordering favourites: dragging now follows the finger and no longer brings back removed apps. Tokyo theme: pick the accent (from the phone, a custom colour, the battery level, or off); the top band no longer shows as a white strip on phones with greyscale colours.
