Word of the day list (assets/vocab.tsv)

The list is built by tools/make_vocab.py from these sources, selected, trimmed
and rearranged (one meaning per word and language; stress marks removed from
Russian, Ukrainian and Lithuanian; Mandarin converted to simplified characters).
As a work derived from CC BY-SA material, vocab.tsv is shared under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

JLPT word lists (which words belong to N5, N4 and N3)
  Jonathan Waller, JLPT Resources, https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/
  Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)
  via open-anki-jlpt-decks by Jamie Sinclair,
  https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks, MIT licence

Readings and parts of speech
  JMdict, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG),
  https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  via jmdict-simplified, https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified

Translations
  Wiktionary, the free dictionary, https://en.wiktionary.org/
  (translation tables of the English entries), by Wiktionary contributors
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0

Simplified Chinese conversion
  OpenCC (opencc-python-reimplemented), Apache License 2.0
  (used when building the list; not part of the app)

The first 60 words were translated by hand for earlier versions of Kamei and
keep those translations.
